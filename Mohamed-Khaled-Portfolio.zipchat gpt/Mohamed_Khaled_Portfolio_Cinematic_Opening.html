01 — About

A closer look at my story

I'm a graphic designer and creative professional with a hands-on background in branding, advertising, and digital design. My work sits at the intersection of clear communication and visual craft — I like taking an idea that's hard to explain and giving it a shape people remember.

Over the past few years I've worked across social media campaigns, brand identities, and web experiences, building a habit of designing with purpose rather than decoration. I care about typography, color systems, and the small details that make a design feel intentional.

Looking ahead, my goal is to grow this practice into a small creative studio — a place where brands can come for design that's considered from the first sketch to the final pixel.
